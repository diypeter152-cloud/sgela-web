1. About the service

Sgela AI (“Sgela”, “we”, “us”) is an AI-powered tutoring service operated by SKOLO EDTECH (Pty) Ltd (registration number 2026/848084/07), a company registered in South Africa. Sgela helps South African Grade 10–12 learners with their schoolwork across subjects aligned to the CAPS curriculum, including Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Geography, English and Afrikaans. Learners access Sgela through WhatsApp and through our website, where they can ask questions by text or by sending a photo of a problem and receive step-by-step explanations.

2. Eligibility

Sgela is intended for high-school learners and those supporting them. Learners under the age of 18 should use Sgela with the awareness and permission of a parent or guardian. By using Sgela you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and that you are permitted to use the service.

3. Subscription terms

  • Free trial: New users receive a 7-day free trial to evaluate the service before paying anything.
  • Price: After the trial, access costs R50 per month (fifty South African Rand), inclusive of any applicable VAT.
  • What you get: An active subscription gives the learner unlimited tutoring questions (fair-use applies) across all supported subjects, by text and photo, for the paid period.
  • Term: Each payment grants 30 days of access. The subscription is month-to-month with no fixed-term contract and no lock-in.

4. Billing

  • Payments are processed securely by PayFast, a licensed South African payment gateway. We never see or store your card or banking details.
  • Learners pay R50 to activate or renew a 30-day period, by card or instant EFT.
  • Billing is month-to-month. A new period begins only when the learner chooses to renew — there is no silent or automatic charge without the learner initiating payment.
  • A confirmation is shown once a payment succeeds, and access is unlocked immediately.

5. Cancellation policy

  • You may cancel at any time, with no cancellation fee and no notice period.
  • Because billing is month-to-month and not automatically recurring, cancelling simply means not renewing — you will not be charged again.
  • When you cancel, your access continues until the end of the 30-day period you have already paid for, after which it stops.
  • To cancel, simply stop renewing, or message us (details below) and we will confirm.

6. Refund policy

  • We provide a 7-day free trial so you can fully evaluate Sgela before paying anything.
