Privacy Policy

Sgela AI · Last updated 24 October 2026

Sgela AI (“Sgela”, “we”, “us”) is an AI tutoring service for South African Grade 10–12 learners, operated by SKOLO EDTECH (Pty) Ltd. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Sgela on WhatsApp or on our website, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number — used to identify you, manage your free trial and subscription, and send you replies.
  • Your messages — the questions you type and any photos of schoolwork you send, so the tutor can answer them.
  • Usage data — basic counts such as how many questions you’ve asked and your trial/subscription status.
  • Payment status — whether a subscription payment succeeded. Card and banking details are handled entirely by our payment provider; we never see or store them.

How we use it

  • To provide tutoring answers to your questions.
  • To manage your 7-day free trial and monthly subscription.
  • To keep short-term conversation context so answers follow on sensibly.
  • To improve the quality and reliability of the service.

Service providers we share data with

We use trusted providers purely to run the service, and share only what each needs:

  • Meta (WhatsApp) — delivers messages between you and Sgela.
  • OpenAI — generates tutoring answers from your questions. Messages are processed to produce a reply.
  • PayFast — processes subscription payments securely.
  • Upstash / Vercel — host the service and store your account status and short conversation history.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data retention

Recent conversation context is kept only briefly and expires automatically. Your account status is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your data at any time using the contact details below.

Children’s privacy

Sgela is designed for high-school learners. Learners under 18 should use Sgela with the awareness and permission of a parent or guardian. We collect only what is needed to provide tutoring.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, in line with South Africa’s POPIA. Message us on WhatsApp or email us and we’ll assist.
